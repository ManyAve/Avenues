# Avenue

Avenue is ManyAve's design system. It holds the look of the ManyAve app as shared tokens and
components, built on ui-sdk and shadcn and shown here in Storybook.

Use the theme switch in the toolbar to see every story in light and dark.

- **Tokens**: colour, type, spacing, radius and elevation values.
- **Foundations**: how those tokens combine, such as layout, nesting and focus.
- **Components**: single controls such as buttons, inputs and selects.
- **Composites**: components put together, such as a card header with a year select.

All example data in Avenue is fictional.
