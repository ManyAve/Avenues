# Composites

Components put together the way the ManyAve app uses them.

- **Form dialog**: every form in a dialog. Icon and title, fields, an error slot, Cancel and submit, and the discard
  guard: with unsaved input, Esc, an outside press, Cancel or the close button ask "Discard changes?" first.
- **Pending confirm dialog**: a confirm for a step that sends something or moves money. While it runs, focus holds on
  the status line; if the reply is lost, it says "couldn't confirm" and offers Try again or the place to check
  (UX.md's two wordings).
